ScalePath SaaS: Pricing Pivot & Channel Optimization

Advising an enterprise SaaS platform on scaling from $2M to $10M ARR through tier realignment and value pricing.

+22% Projected ARPU
-14% Chur Rate Gap
01

The Challenge

Underpriced enterprise tier leading to negative marginal returns relative to high customer success workloads. Customer lifecycle margins were shrinking due to high customer acquisition cost (CAC).

02

Strategic Analysis

Conducted cohort analysis of churn drivers and calculated the Lifetime Value (LTV) relative to CAC. Formulated a tier-based pricing structure to capture the enterprise consumer surplus.

03

Outcome & Recommendations

Introduced custom enterprise pricing with SLAs. Recommended reallocation of 25% of marketing spend to high-converting product-led growth (PLG) channels, generating a modeling forecast of +22% ARPU within 8 months.

Operations
Supply Chain

AeroBoutique: Supply Chain & Inventory Turn Strategy

Overhauling inventory frameworks for an omni-channel luxury retailer experiencing high inventory holding costs and supply bottlenecks.

4.5x Inventory Turn Target
-18% Holding Costs
01

The Challenge

Retailer was holding over 120 days of forward inventory, locking up crucial working capital. High warehousing costs ate away at EBITDA margins.

02

Strategic Analysis

Built an ABC inventory matrix and analyzed supply patterns. Found that 15% of low-margin SKUs occupied 50% of shelf space.

03

Outcome & Recommendations

Redefined supply contracts using a Just-In-Time (JIT) replenishment window for category B and C items. Pruned bottom-performing SKUs, freeing up $45,000 in liquid capital and boosting turns from 2.1x to 4.5x.
